Help Center

Everything you need to know about tracking your cinematic life.

Director Roadmaps

Search for any director via the Roadmaps tab. Prestige automatically cross-references their entire filmography with your logged films. Reach 100% to earn a mastery badge.

Cloud Sync & Safety

All watchlists, logs, and roadmaps are securely synced to Cloud Firestore. You can log in from any device, or clear your browser cache, and your data will instantly populate.

Missing Films

Prestige utilizes the global TMDB API. If an obscure indie film or short is missing from your search, add it to TMDB directly. It will appear on Prestige within 24 hours.

Vibe Search (Coming Soon)

Standard genres are obsolete. We are currently building a Pacing and Mood slider system that lets you filter your Watchlist by exact atmospheric conditions.

Profile Favorites

Navigate to your Profile tab and click any of the empty dashed boxes under "Favorite Films" or "Favorite Shows" to pin your ultimate masterpieces to your personal shelf.

Spoiler Protection

When writing a review on a media page, simply check the "Contains Spoilers" box before saving. Prestige will automatically flag your review in the community feed.

Curated Lists

Head to the Lists tab to create custom collections (e.g., "Best Sci-Fi of the 90s"). You can add films directly from their media pages using the "Add to list" dropdown menu.

TV Episode Tracking

Unlike movies, TV series are tracked on an episode-by-episode basis. Scroll down on any Series page to rate individual episodes, mark them watched, or write specific reviews.
